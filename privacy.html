Privacy Policy.

Last updated: November 2, 2026 · Draft for review before launch

This draft describes the intended approach for REDShop. It must be reviewed and completed by the business owner before the live service collects personal information.

Information we expect to collect

A live REDShop account may use your name and email address to provide account access, order records and digital coupon delivery. Payment details should be handled by the payment provider; REDShop should not store card details.

How information is used

Information should be used to operate accounts, process orders, deliver purchases, provide customer support, prevent fraud and meet applicable legal obligations.

Storage and service providers

The planned service uses Firebase and Cashfree. Before launch, specify the exact data stored, retention periods, processing locations, provider terms and contact for privacy requests.

Your choices and contact

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact hello@redshop.hindcraft.online. Add the final business identity and any required local disclosures before publishing this policy.
